# PhantomOperator: Terms of Use and Acceptable Use

By calling any PhantomOperator endpoint you agree to these terms.

## Permitted use
- **threat-scan** and **opsec-score** may only be run on **yourself**, or on a person or organization that has **explicitly authorized you** to assess their exposure (for example, a client engagement). Every request must include `"consent": true`, which is your confirmation of this.
- **metadata-audit** may be run on web pages you own or operate, or on public pages for legitimate privacy and security review.

## Prohibited use
- Stalking, harassment, doxxing, or locating, tracking or profiling anyone without their consent.
- Building dossiers on private individuals, or reselling results about third parties.
- Any use that violates applicable law, including privacy and data-protection laws (GDPR, CCPA and similar) and anti-stalking laws.
- Attempting to probe private networks, overload the service, or evade rate limits.

## What the service does
- It only reads **publicly available** web data, via DuckDuckGo HTML search and direct HTTP fetches of the URL you supply. It does not access breach databases, data-broker accounts, or anything behind a login.
- Results are heuristic and may be incomplete or wrong. Nothing here is legal advice.
- Requests are processed in memory and are not stored. Standard server logs (IP address, path, status) may be kept briefly for abuse prevention.

## Payments
- Paid calls use x402 (USDC on Base mainnet). Payment is settled only after a successful response. Invalid input and server errors are not charged.
- All payments are final.

## Limits and enforcement
- Per-IP rate limits apply. Abusive callers may be blocked without notice.
- The service is provided "as is", with no warranty and no uptime guarantee. To the maximum extent permitted by law, the operator is not liable for how results are used.
